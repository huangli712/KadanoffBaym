# KB vs NESSi 2.0 benchmark record, 2026-11-24

Machine load during the run: load average ~8 on 18 cores
(`threeband_dimer` + a rustc job on 2 cores); single-threaded
benchmarks, ratios have been stable across load windows.

Commands: `python3 .nessi/bench/compare_kernels.py --rounds 3`
and `python3 .nessi/bench/compare_e2e.py`; reports
`bench-kernels-20261124-1634.md`, `bench-e2e-20261124-1636.md`.
KB side rebuilt from HEAD (`7f8b72a`, crate 0.7.4, release);
NESSi binaries are the prebuilt ones linked against
`.nessi/ppsc-port/nessi-install/lib/libcntr.so`.
