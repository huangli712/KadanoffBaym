(`threeband_dimer` + a rustc job on 2 cores); single-threaded

and `python3 .nessi/bench/compare_e2e.py`; reports

`.nessi/ppsc-port/nessi-install/lib/libcntr.so`.

## Instruction counts and IPC (perf, pinned to CPU 0 P-core)

`python3 .nessi/bench/perf_compare.py --rounds 2` (report
`bench-perf-20261124-1704.md`). Both sides counted with the
`cpu_core` PMU; retired instructions are deterministic and
load-independent (repeat runs agree to <0.01%), cycles/IPC were
taken while the machine still had other jobs on 2 of 18 cores.

| kernel | KB instr | NESSi instr | instr ratio | KB IPC | NESSi IPC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| conv d=1 | 5.98e9 | 5.62e9 | 1.06x | 4.33 | 4.97 |
| conv d=2 | 3.67e10 | 5.60e10 | 0.66x | 2.07 | 4.91 |
| conv d=4 | 1.63e11 | 2.59e11 | 0.63x | 2.41 | 5.37 |
| dyson d=1 | 6.28e9 | 7.22e9 | 0.87x | 2.41 | 4.10 |
| dyson d=2 | 2.82e10 | 5.59e10 | 0.50x | 2.16 | 4.57 |
| dyson d=4 | 1.35e11 | 2.49e11 | 0.54x | 2.46 | 5.14 |
| dyson d=8 | 1.70e12 | 1.68e12 | 1.01x | 4.55 | 4.04 |
| vie2 d=1 | 6.07e9 | 7.19e9 | 0.85x | 3.50 | 4.80 |
| vie2 d=2 | 2.27e10 | 2.19e10 | 1.04x | 2.01 | 2.95 |
| vie2 d=4 | 1.29e11 | 1.16e11 | 1.12x | 2.58 | 3.38 |

Reading:

* KB executes far FEWER instructions wherever the blocks are matrix
  sized: 50-54% of NESSi for dyson d=2/d=4 and 63-66% for conv
  d=2/d=4, 85-87% at d=1 for the dyson/vie2 kernels. The wall-time
  gap is therefore NOT instruction count: it is IPC. KB runs at
  2.0-2.6 instructions per cycle where NESSi's Eigen kernels run at
  4.6-5.4. The remaining work is code generation quality inside
  `mul_acc`/block loops (bounds checks, vectorization, dependency
  chains), not algorithmic.
* dyson d=8 is the case where KB also wins on IPC (4.55 vs 4.04)
  with equal instruction counts -> the 0.96x wall-time win.
* conv d=1 is the only kernel where KB executes more instructions
  (1.06x); its 1.65x wall-time ratio from the timed sweep is
  measurement noise: the perf cycles ratio is 1.22x. Instruction
  counts are the load-independent number to trust.
* vie2 is the only family where KB does not have the instruction
  advantage (1.04-1.12x at d=2/d=4) AND has lower IPC (2.0-2.6 vs
  2.9-3.4) -> consistently the weakest kernel, consistent with the
  timed sweep.
